There are two ways to get here: by public transport, or by driving in.

🚌 Public Transport
MRT: Tanah Merah MRT Station, Exit 2
Bus stop: Tanah Merah Stn Exit 2 (right outside the MRT exit) — served by buses 2, 9, 9A, 12, 12e, 14, 14A, 24, 31, 35, 35M, 38, 45, 47, 48
You can walk in via the front gate (see Driving In below, from the vending machines onwards) or the back gate.

🚗 Driving In
Tell the guard at the gate you're visiting Block 66, #09-30 to get let in.
Park at B2 — go down past B1, and take the first white (visitor) lots you see. Park as close to the stairs as you can.
Go up the staircase — you'll reach a drop-off point with 2 vending machines.
🏊 From the Drop-off Point
Whether you drove in or walked in via the front gate, the directions are the same from here.
Walk towards the swimming pool — the Function Room is at the end of the pool.
Keep walking to the end of the pool — the Function Room is the pitch-black room shown below.
