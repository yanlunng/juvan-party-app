🚪 Back Gate Route (from Tanah Merah MRT)
Quick summary: go through Sceneca, past Ya Kun, cross the zebra crossing, back gate is on your right, scan the entry QR code, stairs on the left, past the gym, and you're at the Function Room. Full step-by-step photos below.
1. Start at the platform.
2. Go down the escalator.
3. Take this exit.
4. Go up the escalator.
5. Go down the steps.
6. Walk straight, following the signs towards Sceneca.
7. You'll reach Sceneca's bicycle drop-off point — walk through it.
8. Turn right after Chagee.

10. This is the main entrance — walk all the way through to the end.
11. The end of the walkway is marked by Ya Kun.
12. Walk to the zebra crossing — the condo is on your right.
13. This is the back gate — scan the entry QR code to open it.
14. Take the staircase on the left.
15. Walk straight through this path.
16. You'll reach the gym — walk past it, and you're already at the end of the pool. The Function Room is the pitch-black room shown below.
🚗🚶 Front Gate (Driving or Walking In)
At the guardhouse, the right gantry is for visitors, the left is for residents — go through the right one. Tell the guard you're here for the Function Room. If asked whose guest you are, it's Block 66, #09-30.
Driving directions (postal code 465532):

Past the guardhouse, you can either drop off at the roundabout, or turn right to drive straight into the carpark.
Once down the ramp, make a hard left here to head down to another ramp.
Down that ramp, turn left straightaway before going down a third ramp, shown below — that's where visitor parking is.
You can park anywhere along that row. Where you end up parking decides which door you take up — both lead to the Function Room.
🅰️ Door 1 — parked near the start of the row
Parking straight down that third ramp leads you to the first entrance — take the stairs up near the 2 vending machines.
Walk towards the swimming pool — the Function Room is at the end of the pool.
Keep walking to the end of the pool — the Function Room is the pitch-black room shown below.
🅱️ Door 2 — parked at the far end of the row (pool deck)
This door is easy to miss — look for the "Staircase to Pool Deck" sign.
Go up the stairs.
You'll come out onto a garden walkway.
The BBQ pit will be on your right.
Walk towards Block 62/64 — you'll see a long walkway.
At the end of the walkway is the AV room, right beside the Function Room — the window with the curtains is the Function Room.
