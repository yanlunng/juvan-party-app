📍 Getting Here

Everything you need to find us
📍 Venue
Casa Merah Function Room
There are two ways to get here: by public transport, or by driving in.
🚪 Jump to Entry QR Code
🚌 Public Transport
MRT: Tanah Merah MRT Station (EW4), Exit 2
Bus stop: Tanah Merah Stn Exit 2 (right outside the MRT exit) — served by buses 2, 9, 9A, 12, 12e, 14, 14A, 24, 31, 35, 35M, 38, 45, 47, 48
From here you can walk in via the Front Gate or the Back Gate — see below.
🚗🚶 Front Gate (Driving or Walking In)
Tell the guard at the gate you're here for the Function Room. If asked whose guest you are, it's Block 66, #09-30.
If driving, park at B2 — go down past B1, and take the first white (visitor) lots you see. Park as close to the stairs as you can.
Whether you drove in or walked in via the front gate, go up the staircase — you'll reach a drop-off point with 2 vending machines.
🚪 Back Gate Route
Photos coming soon — text directions below.
From Tanah Merah MRT Exit 2 / the bus stop, go through Sceneca and walk past Ya Kun. Cross the zebra crossing — the back gate will be on your right.
Scan the entry QR code to open the back gate, then go up the staircase on the left — it leads to the gym. Walk past the gym and you'll see the Function Room.
🏊 From the Drop-off Point (Front Gate)
This continues the Front Gate route above.
Walk towards the swimming pool — the Function Room is at the end of the pool.
Keep walking to the end of the pool — the Function Room is the pitch-black room shown below.
